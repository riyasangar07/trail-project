About Our Institute

Yashwantrao Chavan Polytechnic (YCP) under DKTE Society is located in Ichalkaranji, Maharashtra. It has been a pioneer in imparting technical skills and knowledge since its inception. Approved by AICTE & affiliated with MSBTE, YCP offers seven premier engineering diploma streams.

Our state-of-the-art laboratries, highly certified faculty, and extensive tie-ups with key manufacturing and technological firms ensure students receive top-tier, industry-focused qualifications and robust placement achievements.
